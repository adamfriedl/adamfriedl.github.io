Software engineer and lawyer

I spent a decade in legal services and access to justice. I represented families in housing, benefits, and custody cases, then moved into building programs: I designed a volunteer lawyer program that served over 20,000 defendants in consumer debt court, directed programs at the country's first community court, and worked with jurisdictions across the U.S. to reduce jail populations as part of the MacArthur Foundation's Safety and Justice Challenge. Then I spent eight years engineering at Rakuten Advertising, from junior engineer to leading a squad. I built customer-facing and backend services, moved them onto Kubernetes on Google Cloud, built the CI/CD pipelines that ship them, and most recently built AI agents the operations team used to investigate production incidents.

In both careers my job has been making things happen: deciding how something should work, bringing along the people who depend on it, and owning the result. Now I want to bring the two together, working on systems that have an outsized effect on people's lives and making them work better.

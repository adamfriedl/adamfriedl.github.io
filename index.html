Adam Friedl

Software engineer and lawyer

I spent a decade in legal services and access to justice, representing families in housing, benefits, and custody cases and building programs for people without lawyers. Then I spent eight years engineering at Rakuten Advertising, from junior engineer to leading a squad, most recently building AI agents used in production.

What ties it together is working on systems that have an outsized effect on people's lives, and making them work better.
